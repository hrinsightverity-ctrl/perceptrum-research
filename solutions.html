SOLUTIONS & MODES

Collection modes for every access vector.

Different cohorts need different routes in. We keep incidence feasible without contaminating the sample frame.

CAWI

Programmatic web invites, device optimization, live speeder gating, UID encryption. Integrates Qualtrics, Decipher, Confirmit.

CATI

Native-language interviewers, 100% audio verification, appointment redial, ESOMAR-aligned dialing.

P2W

Phone verify credentials, then push unique survey links. High completion among gatekeepers.

CAPI

Tablet intercepts, CLT and sensory work, GPS stamps, offline capture with encrypted sync.

Hybrid mixed-mode

CAWI + CATI on the same quota frame. SHA-256 respondent IDs for de-dupe. Pacing shifts between desks to fill low-incidence cells. Zero mode-distortion guarantee on the unified file.
